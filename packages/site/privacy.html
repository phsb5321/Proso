Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 16, 2026 · Last Updated: February 16, 2026

How VoxPage Handles Your Data

VoxPage is designed with privacy as a core principle. The extension processes text content locally in your browser. No text, audio, or personal data is ever sent to VoxPage servers unless you are using managed credits (paid subscription).

Extension Data (All Users)

  • Text extraction: When you activate VoxPage on a webpage, the extension extracts text content directly in your browser. This text never leaves your device unless sent to a TTS provider for audio generation.
  • API keys: If you use Bring Your Own Key (BYOK), your API keys are stored locally in your browser using browser.storage.local. They are never transmitted to VoxPage servers or any party other than your selected TTS provider.
  • Audio cache: Generated audio is cached locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage to avoid redundant API calls. This data stays on your device.
  • Settings and preferences: Your playback speed, voice selection, and other preferences are stored locally in your browser.

Data Sent to TTS Providers

When you generate audio, the text content of the paragraphs you listen to is sent to your selected TTS provider (OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Groq, Cartesia, or your browser's built-in engine). This transmission is direct from your browser to the provider. VoxPage does not proxy, log, or store this data. Each provider's privacy policy governs how they handle your text data.

Managed Credit Users (Paid Subscriptions)

If you subscribe to a paid VoxPage plan and use managed credits (rather than BYOK), the VoxPage server processes your requests as follows:

  • Text processing: Text you submit for TTS synthesis is sent to the VoxPage server, which forwards it to the appropriate TTS provider. We do not store the text content after the audio has been generated and delivered.
  • Credit tracking: We track the number of characters processed per billing cycle to manage your credit allocation. This tracking data includes character counts, timestamps, and your account identifier. It does not include the text content itself.
  • Account data: Your email address and subscription status are stored for billing and account management purposes.
  • Data retention: Credit usage data is retained for the duration of your subscription plus 30 days after cancellation. Account data is retained as required for financial record-keeping.

Cookies and Tracking

This website uses zero cookies. There are no tracking scripts, no analytics services, and no third-party advertising on this site.

The VoxPage extension collects no browsing data. It does not track which websites you visit, what content you read, or how you use your browser. The extension activates only when you explicitly click the VoxPage icon or use a keyboard shortcut.

We believe privacy should be the default, not a premium feature. VoxPage practices what it preaches.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your personal data for certain purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

For BYOK users (free tier): We hold no personal data about you. Your API keys and preferences are stored only in your browser. There is nothing for us to delete or provide.

For managed credit users: Contact us at privacy@voxpage.com to exercise any of these rights. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

VoxPage is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.
