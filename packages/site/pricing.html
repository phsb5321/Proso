Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

BYOK is available on every tier without a Proso subscription. Your provider may charge for synthesis. Keys are stored in your browser; for requests routed through Proso, the server uses the key for that request without retaining it.

  • OpenAI — High-quality voices with natural intonation

  • Groq — Blazing-fast inference at low cost
  • Cartesia — Low-latency streaming voices

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I run out of credits?

When your managed credits run out, managed voices pause until your next allocation. You can keep listening with your own API key (BYOK) on any tier — it never spends managed credits.

Can I use my own API keys?

Yes, on every tier, including Free. Proso charges no subscription fee for BYOK; your provider may charge for usage. Bring your own OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Groq, or Cartesia API key and use premium voices without spending any managed credits. Your keys are cached locally in your browser and securely forwarded through our server when you play audio. Keys are used for one request and immediately discarded — never logged or stored.

How do I install the extension?

Install Proso from Mozilla Add-ons in Firefox 109 or later. Before listening, configure your provider API key, a synthesis host you operate, or an eligible managed plan.

What's your refund policy?

Full refund within 7 days for annual plans, no questions asked. Monthly plans are prorated — you'll receive a refund for the unused portion of your billing period.

Do managed credits carry over?

No — unused managed credits do not carry into the next billing cycle. Your full allocation is available until the cycle ends.
