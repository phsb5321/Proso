Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I run out of credits?

Your extension automatically falls back to your browser's built-in text-to-speech engine, which is always unlimited and free. You can also use your own API keys (BYOK) to access premium voices without spending credits.

Can I use my own API keys?

Yes, on every tier — including Free — and it's always free. Bring your own OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Groq, or Cartesia API key and use premium voices without spending any managed credits. Your keys are stored locally in your browser and never sent to VoxPage servers.

Is there a free trial?

The Free tier is unlimited forever — it's not a trial. You get unlimited browser TTS, word-level highlighting, and BYOK support permanently. Paid tiers include a 7-day free trial so you can try managed credits and premium features risk-free.

What's your refund policy?

Full refund within 7 days for annual plans, no questions asked. Monthly plans are prorated — you'll receive a refund for the unused portion of your billing period.

Do unused credits roll over?

Pro and Multilingual tiers allow up to 100,000 characters to roll over to the next billing period. Basic tier credits reset each month. Credits never expire mid-billing cycle.
