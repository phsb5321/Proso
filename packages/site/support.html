Where your support goes

  • GPU time and local speech-model runs used in quality testing.

Support is a donation: it does not buy credits, unlock features, or replace the Paddle subscription — it simply supports the builder.

Quick questions

Donation or subscription?
Donation. It grants no credits and changes nothing in your plan. The Pro subscription stays on the site.
Can I support from outside Brazil?
Yes — use Ko-fi or GitHub Sponsors. PIX works only from Brazilian accounts.
Do I get an invoice?
Donations generate no invoice and no commercial consideration. For commercial licensing, contact commercial@proso.com.br.

Prefer not to donate right now? No problem — keeping on reading is support enough. 💜
