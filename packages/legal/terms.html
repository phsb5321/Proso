3. The Service

VoxPage is a browser extension that converts web page text into spoken audio using text-to-speech technology. It offers multiple modes of operation:

  • Browser TTS: Uses your browser’s built-in speech synthesis engine. Browser TTS is always unlimited and free on all tiers.
  • Managed TTS: Uses VoxPage’s server to process text through premium TTS providers. Managed TTS consumes credits from your subscription.
  • BYOK: Uses your own API keys from third-party TTS providers. BYOK is always available on all tiers, always free, and your API keys never leave your browser.

Core Guarantees

  • The Free Tier is always available without creating an account or providing payment information.
  • Browser-native text-to-speech is always unlimited and incurs no charges.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) is available on all subscription tiers at no additional cost.
  • Word-level synchronization and highlighting is a free feature available on all tiers.

Service Modifications

VoxPage may modify, suspend, or discontinue any part of the service with reasonable notice. Where changes materially affect paid subscribers, VoxPage will provide at least 30 days notice and offer the option to cancel without penalty.

BYOK is available on all subscription tiers, including the Free Tier, at no additional cost. You do not need an account to use BYOK.

Your API keys are stored exclusively in your browser’s local storage (browser.storage.local). VoxPage never transmits your API keys to VoxPage servers. API key data flows only between your browser and the third-party provider you have selected.
